How it works

  1. Choose a password or passphrase.
  2. Adjust the options if needed.
  3. Generate and copy the result.

Questions

Is the result random?

Yes. The generator uses the Web Crypto API and rejection sampling, which avoids modulo bias.

Does UniTools keep my password?

No. Generation and copying happen in your browser. Nothing is stored or sent.
