How it works

  1. Paste Markdown or choose a local file.
  2. Press Convert to PDF.
  3. Download the generated PDF.

Questions

Does the PDF keep Markdown styling?

Headings become title-sized text and Markdown markers are removed. Complex layouts are not rendered.
