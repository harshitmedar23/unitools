    Supported inputs: TXT, Markdown, JSON, CSV and XML. Results download as a ZIP.

    How it works

    1. Choose supported files.
    2. Press Convert files.
    3. Download one ZIP of the results.

    Questions

    Can this convert office files?

    No. DOCX, PPTX, XLSX and EPUB are not listed because no suitable local libraries are vendored here.
