Merge PDF tool

    Add at least 2 PDF files.

    Download merged.pdf

    How it works

    1. Add your PDF files.
    2. Move them into the order you want.
    3. Press Merge PDF and download the result.

    Questions

    Is my file uploaded anywhere?

    No. The merge happens inside your browser. The file never leaves your device.

    Is there a limit?

    No daily limit and no signup. Very large files depend on your device's memory.
