HTML to PDF

The browser print dialog handles the PDF export. External resources may not be available offline.

How it works

  1. Paste HTML or choose a local HTML file.
  2. Press Print / save PDF.
  3. Choose Save as PDF in your browser dialog.

Questions

Does this upload my HTML?

No. The browser opens the content locally and handles printing on your device.
