# 08 Copy and labels

## Voice
A helpful senior who knows the tool: plain, short, specific. Say what happens, not how great it is. No exclamation marks, no emoji, no marketing words (see the list in 02). Use "you". Active verbs.

## Fixed labels (use exactly)
- Browser tools: **Files never leave your device.**
- Sharing tools: **Encrypted and auto-deleted. Not permanent storage.**
- Quick share without password: **Protected by the code. Deleted after 10 minutes.**
- Clean AI code: **Removes invisible characters and tidies formatting. It cannot remove hidden statistical watermarks in text.**
- Resize image presets: **Check your exam notice for the exact size.**
- Compress PDF strong mode: **Pages become images. Text can no longer be selected.**

## Buttons
Name the action: "Merge PDF", "Compress image", "Download merged.pdf", "Choose files", "Start over", "Copy". Never "Submit", "Go", "Click here".

## Drop zone text
"Drop PDF files here" / "or" / [Choose files]. Under it, small: "PDF only. Up to 100 MB in total."

## Progress and results
- "Merging 3 of 5"
- "Saved 412 KB (68% smaller)"
- "Done. 12 pages, 2.4 MB."

## Errors: say what happened and what to do
| Bad | Good |
|---|---|
| "An error occurred." | "This PDF is locked. Remove its password and try again." |
| "Invalid file." | "Merge PDF needs PDF files. For images, use JPG to PDF." |
| "Failed." | "That file is over 50 MB. Choose a smaller one." |
| "Something went wrong!" | "Could not read this image. Try saving it as JPG first." |

## Page titles and descriptions
- Title: "Compress image to 50 KB online, free: UniTools"
- Description (one line): "Reduce a photo to 50, 100 or 200 KB for exam forms. Runs in your browser, nothing is uploaded."

## Home page line
"Free tools for files, images, text and numbers. Nothing is uploaded unless it says so."

## Footer
"Privacy and acceptable use  |  Request a tool or report a bug  |  Made by [your name]"

## Words to avoid
supercharge, unleash, seamless, effortless, powerful, revolutionary, next-gen, cutting-edge, game-changer, elevate, unlock, magic, AI-powered (unless it truly is).
