Base64 encoder and decoder

How it works

  1. Choose encode or decode.
  2. Enter UTF-8 text or valid Base64.
  3. Run the conversion and copy the result.

Questions

Does it support non-English text?

Yes. Encoding and decoding use UTF-8 through TextEncoder and TextDecoder.

What is URL-safe Base64?

It replaces plus and slash with URL-safe characters and omits padding when encoding.
